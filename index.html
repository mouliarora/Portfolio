Roots & Identity

My Hometown: Roorkee

A small city in Uttarakhand with a legendary engineering legacy — the place that shaped my curiosity and ambition.

Where Engineering History Lives

Roorkee is a historic city nestled in the Haridwar district of Uttarakhand, at the foothills of the Himalayas. It is best known as the home of IIT Roorkee — one of India's oldest and most prestigious engineering institutions, established in 1847 as the Thomason College of Civil Engineering.

Growing up surrounded by this rich academic heritage, I was naturally drawn to technology and engineering. The culture of Roorkee instilled in me a deep respect for knowledge, hard work, and the idea that great engineers can come from small cities.

Roorkee is also famous for the Upper Ganges Canal, a marvel of 19th-century civil engineering, and its proximity to the spiritual city of Haridwar and the scenic beauty of Uttarakhand's mountains.

  • 🏔️Located at the foothills of the Shivalik Himalayas
  • 🎓Home to IIT Roorkee (est. 1847) — Asia's oldest technical institution
  • 🌊Famous for the historic Upper Ganges Canal system
  • 🛕Just 30 km from the sacred city of Haridwar
  • 💚A city that values education, discipline, and ambition
